About Us

Gauteng Community Food Support was founded to address food insecurity in our communities. We believe that every person deserves access to nutritious food, regardless of their situation.

Our team works with local suppliers, volunteers, and community leaders to collect, pack, and distribute food to those who need it most. We are proud to serve communities across Gauteng from townships to inner-city neighbourhoods.

Our Mission

To provide immediate food relief while building long-term food security through education, sustainable growing projects, and community empowerment.

Our Vision

A Gauteng where every family has reliable access to enough nutritious food to live a healthy, active life with dignity and hope.

Our Values

We are guided by compassion, integrity, transparency, and community partnership. We treat everyone with respect and believe that together, we can end hunger in our province
