Our Services

We offer a range of food support programmes to help individuals and families across Gauteng through the year.

Monthly Food Parcels

Registered households receive a box of non-perishables, fresh produce, and basic essentials every month.

Daily Community Meals

Hot, nutritious meals served Monday to Saturday at our central feeding points.

School Nutrition

Breakfast and lunch support for learners in under-resourced schools.

Food Donation Drives

We collect surplus food from businesses and redistribute it to where it is needed most.

Garden & Farming Projects

We teach communities how to grow their own food sustainably, creating long-term food security.

Emergency Food Relief

Immediate food assistance for families facing crises, disasters, or unexpected hardship.

Nutrition Education

Workshops on healthy eating, meal planning and making the most of limited resources.

Summer Fresh Produce

In summer, we distribute fresh fruits and vegetables from community gardens that are healthy, seasonal produce at its best.

Winter Warm-up Programme

During cold winter months, we provide warm soups, hot meals, and extra energy-rich foods to help families stay healthy.
